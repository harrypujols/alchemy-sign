The Alchemist Zodiac

From the private pages of the old alchemist's study, a simple, secret pattern unfolds—ten celestial dispositions aligned to the ancient primal elements. Study thy date of birth and uncover thy true alchemical nature.

Calculate Your Alchemical Sigil

“By number, all things are made known. Within thy birth date lies the cipher of thy elemental self.”

To divine one's Alchemical Sign, take the day of thy birth, be it the 4th or the 27th, and reduce it to a single digit by summing its parts. Shouldst thou be born on the 23rd, thou shalt perform: 2 + 3 = 5. If this sacred sum results in zero, consider it 10, the sacred number of unity in duality. Thus: Birth on the 9th → 9 = Azoth Birth on the 30th → 3 + 0 = 3 = Terra Birth on the 10th → 1 + 0 = 1 = Ignis This final digit reveals thy elemental dominion, drawn from the mystic wheel of ten sacred alchemical signs.

Ignis (Fire of Sulfur)

Bold and restless, you burn with creative spark. Your spirit is one of will and transformation—ready to burn away dross and reveal hidden gold within the self.

Aer (Air of Salt)

You are the thinker, the communicator. Agile mind, shifting as vapor—yet ever-reaching for insight, weaving threads of thought like winds shaping clouds.

Terra (Earth of Mercury)

Grounded and sturdy, you embody stability. Like fertile soil, you nurture growth—patient, constant, and unyielding in purpose.

Aqua (Water of Luna)

You harbor depth and reflection. Soft yet powerful, your emotions flow like rivers—calming, cleansing, and profoundly intuitive.

Quinta Essentia

The alchemist's quintessence: you balance above the four—bringing harmony, integration, and spiritual synthesis. You are the bridge between matter and spirit.

Prima Materia (The One)

You are the raw, undifferentiated potential, the cosmic clay of creation. From you, all things emerge and to you, all returns.

Vitriol (Acid of Transformation)

A fierce agent of change—you dissolve the old to forge anew. Your strength lies in alchemical rebirth, purging impurities to achieve clarity.

Lapis (Philosopher's Stone)

You shine with perfected essence. Wise, luminous, and incorruptible—you embody spiritual alchemy made manifest.

Azoth (Universal Solvent)

You permeate all things, connecting and uniting. Versatile and healing, you bring harmony to chaos and unify opposites.

Sol/Luna (Integration of Gold & Silver)

You are the harmonized sun and moon, radiant yet receptive. Balanced between day and night, masculine and feminine, conscious and dream—you stand at the threshold of wholeness.
