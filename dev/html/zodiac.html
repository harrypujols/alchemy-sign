Calculate Your Alchemical Sigil

1 + 2 + 1 + 0 + 1 + 8 + 7 + 5 = 25 = 2 + 5 = 7

If this sacred sum results ends in zero and above the 10th, consider it 10, the sacred number of unity in duality.
